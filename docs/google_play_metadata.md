## Full Description (Max 4000 characters)
Stop wasting pipe on trial-and-error bends. Conduit Bender & EMT Pipe Calc is the fast, offline-first bending calculator built specifically for commercial electricians, industrial electricians, and electrical apprentices.

Calculate exact mark-to-mark distances, shrink allowances, and cut marks in seconds. Read every result in real-world tape-measure fractions down to 1/16"—no mental decimal conversions while standing on a ladder.

CALIBRATED FOR YOUR BENDER
----------------------------------------
Bender shoes vary between manufacturers. The app includes precision presets calibrated for and compatible with Ideal, Klein, Greenlee, and Milwaukee hand benders, as well as Gardner Bender models:
• Calibrated for Ideal® iron and aluminum benders
• Compatible with Klein Tools® cast aluminum and steel series
• Pre-configured for Greenlee® Site-Rite® series
• Compatible with Milwaukee® and Gardner Bender models
• Custom Bender Profiles (Pro): Dial in your own exact take-up, gain, and deduction values for worn shoes or specialty hydraulic benders.

----------------------------------------
CORE BENDING CALCULATORS
----------------------------------------
• Offset Bends: Enter obstruction height and bend angle (10°, 15°, 22.5°, 30°, 45°, 60°). Calculates multiplier spacing and total pipe shrink instantly.
• 90° Stub-Up: Enter desired stub height; automatically applies your shoe's take-up deduction to give the exact first mark.
• Kicked 90° Bends: Combine a 90° bend with an offset kick in a single stick of pipe to navigate complex ceiling and wall obstacles.
• 3-Point Saddle: Clear round obstacles (water lines, conduit racks). Enter obstacle diameter/height and bend angle (45° center with 22.5° sides, or 60°/30°).
• 4-Point Saddle: Clear rectangular ducts and wide obstructions with dual offset calculations.
• Corner & Parallel Offsets: Maintain uniform spacing across multiple concentric conduit runs without doglegs.

----------------------------------------
FIELD-READY FEATURES
----------------------------------------
• Tape Measure Fractions: Formats all lengths as true fractional inches (e.g. 14 3/8", 22 5/16") with 1/16" precision. Metric mode (mm/cm) supported.
• Multi-Conduit Support: Handles EMT (Electrical Metallic Tubing), IMC (Intermediate Metal Conduit), and Rigid heavy-wall steel in 1/2", 3/4", 1", and 1-1/4" trade sizes.
• Trade Reference Tables: Quick access to multipliers, shrink tables, and National Electrical Code (NEC) reference charts.
• Visual Bend Diagrams: Step-by-step graphical guides showing which way to point the bender hook and arrow for each bend.
• Calculation History: Reopen recent runs on the job site with one tap.
